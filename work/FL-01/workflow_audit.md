## 2. Three Target Tasks

### Target Task 1 — Debug Programming Errors

**Task:**  
Use AI to help identify and fix programming errors.

**Done well:**  
The root cause is correctly identified, the proposed fix works when tested, and I can explain why the original code failed.

### Target Task 2 — Learn Difficult CSE/AI-ML Concepts

**Task:**  
Use AI as a study partner to understand difficult technical concepts.

**Done well:**  
I can explain the concept in my own words and solve at least one related problem without copying the AI's answer.

### Target Task 3 — Research Technologies for Projects

**Task:**  
Use AI to help compare technologies and approaches for software projects.

**Done well:**  
I can compare at least two reasonable options using clear criteria, verify important claims, and make a final choice based on the project's requirements.

## 4. How I Will Use AI

AI will be used mainly as a collaborator, reviewer, or first-draft assistant rather than as a replacement for my own judgment. For learning tasks, I will make sure I understand the material myself. For coding and research tasks, I will verify AI-generated suggestions before using them. For important decisions, I will remain responsible for the final decision.
