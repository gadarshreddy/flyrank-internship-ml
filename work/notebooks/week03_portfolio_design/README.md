# Week 3: Portfolio Visual Identity

## Objective
Create a consistent visual identity for my AI/ML portfolio and select visuals that demonstrate real technical work.

## Portfolio Claim
I build practical AI/ML projects in Python and explain the evidence, trade-offs, and next steps clearly.

## Target Audience
AI/ML engineering managers and internship reviewers.

## Content Map
- Home: Introduction, featured projects, skills, and contact.
- Projects: Overview of technical projects and their evidence.
- Medical Paper Summarizer: Problem, implementation, outputs, limitations, and next steps.
- CTR / Engagement Opportunity Scoring: Business question, dataset, task framing, analysis, and next steps.
- About: Academic background, technical interests, and contact information.

| Element | Choice |
|---|---|

### Design Principles
- Keep the interface minimal and readable.
- Use consistent typography, spacing, and colors.
- Prioritize real project evidence over decorative visuals.
- Ensure screenshots are readable and accurately represent the work.

## Image Selection

### Images to Include
- Actual Medical Paper Summarizer screenshots.
- Actual notebook screenshots showing data analysis and task framing.
- A simple portfolio logo and favicon.

### Images to Reject
Generic AI-generated futuristic images that do not demonstrate actual technical work.

### Reason
Real screenshots provide more credible evidence of engineering ability than decorative AI-generated imagery.
