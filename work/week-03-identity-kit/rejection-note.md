# Image Rejection Note

## Rejected direction

A highly decorative, neon-heavy AI background with many glowing elements
and complex gradients.

## Why it was rejected

It draws attention away from project titles, code, and evidence; it also
makes long technical explanations harder to scan. Its visual intensity
conflicts with the portfolio's minimal, content-first identity.

## Decision

Use restrained technical visuals with a charcoal base, readable text,
and small electric-green accents. Use real project screenshots or
evidence-based charts when explaining a specific project.
